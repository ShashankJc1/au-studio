The studio

We design rooms that feel effortless to live in, and we build them with the same care we draw them.

AU Studio is a team of interior designers, site engineers and craftspeople. We take a project from the first measured survey to the last cushion, so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the room.

Our work is calm and material-led: natural light, honest timber, stone and linen, and storage that disappears into the architecture. Every space is shaped around the people who use it.

How we work

From first visit to move-in

Four clear stages, each signed off by you before the next begins. One contract and one point of contact throughout.

  1. Consultation

    We visit, measure and listen: your routines, your must-haves and an honest budget range. The first visit is free.

  2. Design

    Layouts, 3D views and a curated material palette, refined with you until every room feels right. Then a fixed, itemised quote.

  3. Build

    Civil work, joinery and finishes run to a written schedule, with a site lead on hand and weekly photo updates.

  4. Handover

    A detailed snag check, a deep clean and final styling. You receive the keys, the warranty and a home ready to live in.
