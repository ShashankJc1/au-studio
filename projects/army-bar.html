Two design directions for a club bar: a chandeliered lounge and a timber-lined games room.

The first option pairs a coffered ceiling and crystal chandeliers with bottle-green upholstery and a long backlit bar.

The second is warmer and more relaxed: timber-lined walls, a suspended glass rack over the counter and a full-size snooker table at its centre.
