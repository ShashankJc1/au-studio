A neighbourhood salon planned around a calm lounge at the front and a bright styling floor behind it.

Backlit oval mirrors framed in brushed gold, cognac leather styling chairs and a marble-look floor keep the room warm, bright and easy to clean between appointments.

At the entrance, a moss wall with a neon motif gives the salon its signature, and a fluted timber screen separates the waiting lounge from the working floor without closing it off.
