The studio

Designers and builders, one team

AU Studio is a Bengaluru team of interior designers, site engineers and craftspeople. We take a project from the first measured survey to the last cushion, so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the room.

Our story

We started with a simple idea: the people who draw a room should be the people who build it.

Too many projects lose their way between the design and the site. Measurements drift, materials get swapped and the room you signed off is not quite the room you get.

So we keep both under one roof. Your designer and your site lead work together from day one, and the same team that made the 3D views is there when the last shelf goes up.

Est. 2014 · Bengaluru
How we think

Three things we never skip

  • 01

    Listen first

    Every project begins with how you live or work: your routines, your clutter, your light. The design follows from that, not from a catalogue.

  • 02

    Honest materials

    Timber, stone, linen and good hardware, chosen to age well. We explain every option and its cost, so you can decide with confidence.

  • 03

    One team, start to finish

    One designer and one site lead for the whole project. Weekly updates, a written schedule and a single point of contact.
