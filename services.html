Ways to work with us

Choose how much we take on

Both start with the same free consultation. You can move from design only to design and build at any point.

Option one

Design only

For clients with their own contractor, or who want a clear plan before they decide.

  • Measured survey and space planning
  • Photo-real 3D views of every room
  • Material, finish and lighting palette
  • Working drawings for your contractor
  • Two site visits to check progress
Option two

Design & build

Everything in design only, then we build it with our own site team and joinery partners.

  • Fixed, itemised quote before work starts
  • Written schedule with weekly photo updates
  • Dedicated site lead and single point of contact
  • Snag check, deep clean and styling at handover
  • 10-year warranty on joinery
Questions

Good to know

How much does an interior project cost?

It depends on the size of the space, the scope and the materials you choose. After the first consultation we share a budget range, and after design you receive a fixed, itemised quote, so there are no surprises later.

How long does it take?

A single room or kitchen is much quicker than a full home or a commercial fit-out. We agree a written schedule with you before work starts and send photo updates every week.

Will I see the design before work begins?

Yes. Every room is shown in photo-real 3D with the actual materials and finishes, and nothing is built until you have signed it off.

Do you work outside Bengaluru?

Most of our work is in and around Bengaluru. For projects further away, get in touch and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it well.

What warranty do you offer?

Joinery we build carries a 10-year warranty, and we come back to fix any snags that show up in the first months after handover.
