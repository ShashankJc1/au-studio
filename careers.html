Careers

Build beautiful rooms with us

We are a small team that designs and builds, so everyone sees their work through from the first sketch to the finished room. If that sounds like you, we would love to hear from you.

Why AU Studio

A good place to grow

  • See it built

    Designers visit site and site leads sit in on design reviews. You learn the whole craft, not one slice of it.

  • Real responsibility

    Own projects early, with a senior colleague beside you when you need one.

  • Learn every week

    Material visits, supplier workshops and Friday reviews of what went well and what didn't.

  • Fair and clear

    Transparent pay, sensible hours and leave you can actually take.

Join the team

Don't see your role? Send your CV and portfolio to careers@austudio.in and tell us what you'd bring.

Interior DesignerFull time · Bengaluru · 2+ years

Lead residential and commercial projects from brief to handover, working closely with a site lead.

What you'll do

  • Run client meetings, briefs and design presentations
  • Prepare layouts, working drawings and material palettes
  • Coordinate with the 3D, site and joinery teams

What we look for

  • A degree or diploma in interior design or architecture
  • Confident with AutoCAD and SketchUp
  • A portfolio of built work

Site SupervisorFull time · Bengaluru · 3+ years

Keep our sites safe, tidy and on schedule, and make sure what we build matches what we drew.

What you'll do

  • Manage daily work across civil, carpentry, electrical and painting
  • Track the schedule and send weekly photo updates
  • Run snag checks before handover

What we look for

  • Experience supervising interior fit-outs
  • Able to read drawings and measure accurately
  • Clear communication with clients and trades

3D VisualiserFull time · Bengaluru · 1+ years

Turn our designs into photo-real views that help clients see their space before it is built.

What you'll do

  • Model and render interiors from designer drawings
  • Build material and lighting libraries
  • Turn around revisions quickly

What we look for

  • Strong with SketchUp plus Enscape, V-Ray or Lumion
  • An eye for light, materials and composition
  • A portfolio of interior renders
