Questions

Before you reach out

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first meeting at our studio or your site, including measurements, is free with no obligation.

How long does a typical home take?

A 2 or 3BHK full interior usually takes 8 to 12 weeks from design sign-off, depending on scope and civil work.

Do you work outside the city?

We take on homes across the state and commercial projects across India. Travel is quoted separately.

How do payments work?

In milestones tied to visible progress: booking, design sign-off, material delivery and handover.

What does the warranty cover?

Modular woodwork has a 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects, and hardware carries the brand's warranty. Every project includes one free service visit in the first year.
